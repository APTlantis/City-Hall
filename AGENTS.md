# City Hall Instructions

Inherit [drive instructions](D:/AGENTS.md) before using this directory.

## Role

City Hall is the canonical Aptlantis standards and adopted-governance resource. Its active suites govern work across the development drive.

`D:\.city_hall\City Planning` is the separate sandbox for standards proposals, experiments, review, historical lineage, and preservation. City Planning material is non-governing unless deliberately adopted by an active City Hall standard.

## Local Rules

- Treat active City Hall suites as the authority for current work.
- Keep City Planning distinct from the canonical suites; do not let its candidate or historical wording override active standards.
- Record promotion, retirement, and authority-boundary changes in visible manifests, READMEs, adoption notes, or migration records.
- Preserve dated historical evidence rather than rewriting its original paths.
